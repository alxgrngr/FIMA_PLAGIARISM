## Use in a training set

Prefer Indonesian full text dated through 2021 and confirm the article or issue expressly grants an appropriate license. Store DOI, publication date, title, journal, section, the license version and a link to the evidence. Keep the article text and any PDFs outside public Git. Record the publication date as a human-authorship proxy, since publication date alone does not establish authorship.

Use only prose from the discussion and conclusion; drop quotations, tables, author metadata and references. Pair the same topics and approximate section lengths with text generated independently by multiple local models. Keep related articles and prompts in the same split, then measure both detection and false alarms on held-out human and AI material. First validate on lab reports before using the results for those reports.
